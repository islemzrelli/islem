Stories from Gabès

The Evidence

The environmental crisis in Gabès is documented in scientific analyses, institutional reports, and legal records. This archive brings those findings together, making the evidence easier to understand and tracing every claim back to its source.

How findings are labeled and what the badges mean
  • Stated in the source — the document says this explicitly.
  • Summary of the source — a faithful paraphrase.
  • Calculated from source data — arithmetic on reported values, with inputs and method shown.
  • Interpretation — explanation or context added here, not stated by the source.

What the badges mean

  • Measured — values measured in samples or observations.
  • Authors' estimate — a figure the authors calculated or modelled, such as a total or an economic loss.
  • Risk estimate — a calculated possible risk to people. It is not a count of cases.
  • Audit rating — how the audit rated an item against a regulation or safeguard. It is not a court ruling.
  • Reported by residents — what residents told the auditors. It is not medically confirmed.
  • From a press article — what a newspaper article says. It is not independently checked here.
  • Stated limitation — a limit the authors themselves state.
