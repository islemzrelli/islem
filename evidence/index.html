The Evidence

The environmental crisis in Gabès is documented in scientific analyses, institutional reports, and legal records. This archive brings those findings together, making the evidence easier to understand and tracing every claim back to its source.

How findings are labeled
  • Stated in the source — the document says this explicitly.
  • Summary of the source — a faithful paraphrase.
  • Calculated from source data — arithmetic on reported values, with inputs and method shown.
  • Interpretation — explanation or context added here, not stated by the source.
  • Allegation — unresolved — a claim that is reported but not established.
